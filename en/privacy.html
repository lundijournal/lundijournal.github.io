Lundi Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: October 16, 2026

Lundi ("we") is a private journaling app. Our first principle is to keep our hands off your journal. This policy explains, as plainly as we can, what information Lundi uses, where it is stored, and who can see it.

In One Sentence

We do not collect your personal information. Lundi has no accounts and no servers. What you write and add is stored only on your devices and in your own iCloud, and we cannot access it.

1. Where What You Write and Add Is Stored

ContentWhat it is used forWhere it is stored
Entries, photos, letters to the future, special days, journal names and coversShowing them to you; syncing them between your devicesOn your devices; when iCloud Sync is on, also in your own private iCloud storage
Location (optional)Noting the city you are in on an entryOnly the city name is saved, never coordinates or other precise location; stored on your devices and in iCloud
Birth year (optional)Showing "age X that year"Only on this device

Lock, reminder and other settingsMaking the app work the way you likeLock settings are kept in your Keychain like the passcode (synced when iCloud Keychain is on, so they survive reinstalling); reminders and other settings stay only on this device
  • We have no servers. None of this content passes through or is stored on any system of ours.
  • iCloud Sync is provided by Apple. Your data is kept in your own private iCloud storage and encrypted by Apple; we cannot see any of it. You can turn it off at any time in Me › Data & Sync › iCloud Sync. In mainland China, iCloud services for Apple Accounts in mainland China are operated by Guizhou-Cloud Big Data Industry Co., Ltd.   • For stronger protection, you can turn on Advanced Data Protection in iOS Settings.
  • Like data from any other app, Lundi's content is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer). These backups are kept by Apple and by you, and we cannot access them either.

2. Permissions Lundi Asks For

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature that needs it. Declining never stops you from writing.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We don't use any advertising, analytics or tracking tools, or any third-party SDKs.
  • We don't record what you do in the app.
  • We don't sell or share any of your information.
  • We don't lock away what you have written because you haven't paid.

4. Data We Can See

The following data is collected and provided by Apple. We see only aggregated results. We cannot see your journal, and we cannot tell which person any of it comes from:

  • App Store statistics on downloads, updates and purchases;
  • Crash reports and usage analytics that Apple provides if you have agreed in iOS to "Share With App Developers". You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

5. Purchases

Lundi Pro is purchased through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payment information is handled by Apple, and we never receive your payment method or personal details. Apple tells the app whether you have made a purchase; this does not go through us.

6. Information You Send Us

If you email us through About Lundi › Feedback, we receive your email address and what you write. The only things the app attaches automatically are the app version and the iOS version, never anything from your journal. We process these emails only to respond to your request (our legitimate interest in answering you), for no other purpose, and never pass them to anyone else. Emails are kept for at most 12 months after your request has been dealt with and are then deleted; you can ask us to delete them sooner at any time. Emails are transferred to and handled in the country or region where the developer is located, which may be outside your own.

7. Keyboards

You choose which keyboard you write with in iOS. The built-in iOS keyboards do not pass what you type to us or to any third party. If you use a third-party keyboard and have given it "Full Access" in iOS Settings, the text you type is handled by that keyboard under its own privacy policy, which is outside Lundi's control. We suggest using the built-in iOS keyboards when you write.

8. Exported Files

Backups, Markdown and PDF files you export are kept by you and are not protected by Lundi's locks. Please keep them somewhere safe.

9. Your Rights: View, Export, Delete

  • View and export: Everything you have written can be viewed in the app at any time and exported as files in open formats.
  • Delete one entry: A deleted entry first goes to Recently Deleted and is permanently deleted after 30 days. You can also delete it permanently right away from there.
  • Delete everything: Me › Data & Sync › Delete All Entries. When sync is on, this also deletes the same content from your other devices and from iCloud.
  • Correct, add to and move your data: Editing an entry in the app is how you correct or add to it; to take your journal elsewhere, use the backup or Markdown files made by Export.
  • Withdraw consent: Notification, location and camera permissions can be turned off at any time in Settings › Lundi; iCloud Sync is turned off in Me › Data & Sync. What you have already written is not affected.
  • Please note: Deleting the app alone does not delete your entries in iCloud. To delete the data in iCloud, first delete all entries in the app, or manage it in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Because we don't hold any of your data, you do all of this yourself on your own devices. There is no need to contact us.

10. Children

Lundi has no accounts and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are under 14, please read this policy together with a parent or guardian and use Lundi only with their permission.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app's update notes and update the date above. We will never start collecting your information without telling you and getting your consent.
