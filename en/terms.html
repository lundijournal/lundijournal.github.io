Lundi Terms of Use

Last updated and effective: October 13, 2026

1. What These Terms Are

By downloading and using Lundi, you agree to these terms and to Apple's Standard End User License Agreement (EULA). Where the two differ, whichever is more favorable to you applies.

2. Your Content Belongs to You

Everything you write and add in Lundi (entries, photos, letters and so on) belongs to you. We have no servers, and we do not store, view or use your content.

3. Taking Care of Your Content

  • Your content is stored on your devices and in your own iCloud. Please note: if iCloud Sync is off, your content cannot be recovered after you switch phones or lose your phone. Deleting the app alone does not delete your content in iCloud.
  • We suggest saving a copy regularly with Export Journal › Backup.
  • Exported files are not protected by Lundi's locks. Please keep them safe yourself.
  • If you forget your passcode, you can verify with your iPhone passcode and set a new one. Lundi never deletes your entries because of wrong passcode attempts.

4. Lundi Pro

  • Lundi Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription (auto-renewable), or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are as shown in the app and on the App Store.
  • A subscription renews automatically within the 24 hours before the end of the current period, and payment is charged to your Apple Account. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. After you cancel, you can keep using Pro until the end of the current period.
  • Purchases, renewals and refunds are all handled by Apple under Apple's rules. After switching devices or reinstalling, tap "Restore Purchases" on the Lundi Pro page.
  • When Pro ends, everything you have already used stays as it is (for example, an entry with 9 photos, or a cover you have chosen); you just can't add more. What you have written can always be viewed, exported and restored.

5. Fair Use

Please do not reverse engineer Lundi or use it for any unlawful purpose.

6. Disclaimer

We do our best to keep Lundi stable and reliable, but we cannot promise it will always be free of errors. We cannot be responsible for content lost because of device failure, iCloud services, your own deletion or similar causes, which is why we suggest backing up regularly.

If these terms change in a meaningful way, we will tell you in the app's update notes.
