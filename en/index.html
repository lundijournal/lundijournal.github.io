Write quietly

Open it and start writing

Today's date, a short prompt, and below it, a blank page. Add a mood, a photo or the city you're in if you like, or just write a few lines. Nothing gets lost if you stop halfway; it will be there when you come back.

Keep

A year, a book

Each year's entries are gathered into a book of their own. Give it a name, choose a cover, pick a paper you like. A few years from now, your bookshelf will hold the days you've lived.

Each year, choose a cover you love

Look back

Return to any day

Read entry by entry along the Timeline, or tap a day on the full-year Calendar. Looking for something in particular? A quick search takes you there.

Time capsule

Write to your future self, or to someone you love

Write a letter to your future self and choose the day it opens. Until then, it stays sealed and no one can read it. When the day comes, Lundi gently lets you know. After reading, you can write a reply and send it further into the future.

You can also write to a partner, family member or friend. The letter waits in your own letterbox until the day you pick, then goes out as a PDF, an image or an envelope.

Private and secure

Just for you

A passcode of its own

Set a passcode just for Lundi, separate from your phone's. Knowing your phone passcode isn't enough to open it.

Resets leave a record

If someone resets it with Forgot Passcode, Lundi keeps a record you'll see next time.

Three locks

App Lock, Journal Lock and Letterbox Lock, opened with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Hidden when you switch away

When Lundi goes to the background, it covers the screen, so your entries don't show in the app switcher.

Nothing gets lost

What you write stays written

Saves as you go

Saves every second you pause. Unfinished or interrupted entries are kept for you.

No wrong taps

Lundi never asks "Save changes?". When you edit a saved entry, the original stays until you save.

30 days to undo

Deleted entries wait in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

With iCloud Sync on, a new phone or a reinstall brings everything back from your own iCloud.

Always yours

Your journal is always yours

Full backup

An open, widely used format (.zip). Restore everything on a new phone or in another app.

Markdown

Export each entry as a text file you can open anywhere.

Typeset PDF

Laid out like a book, ready to print and bind.

No account to lose or get locked out of. Moving in is easy too: Markdown, plain text and export files from common journaling apps are supported.
